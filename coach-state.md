## Equipment reality — read before programming any weight

Brandon is traveling and has trained at **five different gyms** in three weeks: two Planet Fitness locations, a PF with a squat rack, two hotel gyms, and now **Planet Fitness Eugene**.

- **Smith bars vary and are often unmarked.** ~24–25 lb estimated. Effective resistance can differ 10–20 lb between machines with identical plates. **Program and log PLATES PER SIDE.**
- **Functional trainer cable stacks run a different ratio than standard stacks.** Face pulls that are 60 lb on a standard stack are 37.5–42.5 on a functional trainer. Not a strength change.
- **Cable machine increments vary** — some go 120→135→150, some 160→170 with no 5 lb step.
- **Hotel gyms:** dumbbells only, typically capped at 75 lb, kettlebells at 30 lb, no barbell, no Smith. Brandon improvises well — goblet squats, TRX rows, pull-ups, DB skull crushers.
- **Always write a named fallback into every exercise.** He has been shut out of the leg curl machine twice.
- **Always log which gym.**
